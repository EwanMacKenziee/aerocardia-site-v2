Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

AeroCardia Inc. ("AeroCardia," "we," "us") operates aerocardia.com and the AeroCardia waitlist and pre-order program. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and, for residents of Quebec, the Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector ("Law 25").

Information we collect

We collect information directly from you and, where you use certain features, from the third-party services that power those features:

  • Waitlist sign-up: your name and email address, submitted through our waitlist form (hosted by JotForm Inc.), plus the date and time you signed up.
  • Pre-order deposit: when you reserve a unit with the $60 CAD deposit, your name, email, billing and shipping address, and payment details are collected by our checkout provider, Shopify Inc. (and its payment processor). AeroCardia does not receive or store your full payment card number.
  • Direct contact: anything you send to info@aerocardia.com or through the social channels we operate.
  • Technical data: standard web server logs collected by our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.), such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, used only for site operation and security.

How we use your information

  • To respond to waitlist sign-ups and send updates about availability, pricing and launch timing.
  • To process and fulfill pre-order deposits and keep you informed about the status of your order.
  • To respond to support and general inquiries.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of our website.

We do not use your personal information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you, and we do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with the service providers that help us operate the waitlist, checkout and website — currently JotForm, Shopify (and its payment processor), and Vercel — solely so they can perform those functions on our behalf, and with professional advisors or authorities where required by law. Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing information processed on its platform.

Where your information is processed

AeroCardia is based in Quebec, Canada. Some of the service providers listed above may process or store information on servers located outside Quebec, including in the United States. By using our waitlist or checkout, you consent to this cross-border processing.

Data retention

We retain waitlist and pre-order information for as long as needed to communicate with you about AeroCardia, fulfill your order, and meet our legal and accounting obligations, and no longer. You can ask us to delete your information at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Security

We rely on our service providers’ security measures (encryption in transit, access controls) to protect the information they process on our behalf, and we limit internal access to personal information to the team members who need it to do their jobs.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw your consent to our collection, use or disclosure of your information, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.
  • Request that we cease disseminating your information or de-index it, where applicable.
  • Receive, in a structured format, the personal information you provided us, or have it transferred to another organization, where technically feasible.
  • File a complaint with Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our privacy team at info@aerocardia.com. We will respond within the time limits set out in applicable law.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies. The waitlist and checkout are embedded from JotForm and Shopify respectively; each may set its own cookies when you interact with their forms, under their own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

AeroCardia is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 14. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as AeroCardia’s products and practices evolve. We will post the revised version here with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or requests relating to your personal information, can be sent to the person responsible for the protection of personal information at AeroCardia, reachable at info@aerocardia.com.
